5. REAL-WORLD DATA GAP ANALYSIS & COLLECTION RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
While the current dataset provides a robust academic benchmark, real-world deployment
requires covering practical edge cases. Recommended next collection targets:
• Plastic:   Crushed transparent PET water bottles, opaque shampoo bottles, coloured caps, dirty plastic bags.
• Paper:     Creased receipts, oil-stained cardboard boxes (which should classify as organic or other).
• Glass:     Broken bottle shards, coloured glass (amber, emerald green), laboratory vials.
• Metal:     Crushed soda cans, rusty tins, bottle crowns, aluminum takeout foil trays.
• Organic:   Mouldy leftovers, coffee grounds, eggshells, tea bags, wet food scraps.
• E-Waste:   Swollen lithium batteries, frayed charging cables, discarded earbuds, computer RAM.
• Hazardous: Aerosol spray paint cans, mercury thermometers, expired blister packs, chemical bleach containers.
• Other:     Ceramic mugs, rubber bands, composite chip bags (metallized plastic), cigarette butts.
